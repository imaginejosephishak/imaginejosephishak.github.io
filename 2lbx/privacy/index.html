2LBX Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

2LBX is an app for keeping an inventory of your tools. It is developed by Joseph Ishak ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

In short: 2LBX does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

You can record details about your tools in 2LBX, such as names, makes, models, sizes, purchase dates and places, UPCs and SKUs, warranty details, links, tags, rooms, locations and notes. You can also attach pictures of tools, receipts and manuals.

All of this information is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is not sent to me or to any server I operate. I cannot see it.

Photos and files

When you add a picture, receipt or manual, you choose it yourself using the system photo picker or the Files browser. The app receives only the item you select. It does not browse or read the rest of your photo library or files.

Export and import

If you use Export, the app creates a file containing your inventory and attachments, and you choose where to save it, for example on your device or in iCloud Drive. That file is then stored wherever you put it, under the terms of that service. Import only reads a file you select.

Backups

If you back up your device using iCloud Backup or your computer, Apple's backup may include the app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by me.

What 2LBX does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual tools, rooms or attachments in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Any export files you saved must be deleted separately from wherever you stored them.

Children

2LBX is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
